I hold a Master of Science in Applied Mathematics with a specialization in Data Science from the University of Angers in France, building on a Bachelor’s in Mathematics and the PluriPASS(former PACES) preparatory course. My academic journey, initiated by a scientific baccalaureate in Chad, has provided me with a strong capacity for adaptation and a unique cross-cultural perspective.

I am passionate about implementing AI models to address tangible challenges at the intersection of science and industry. My goal is to contribute to ambitious research and development (R&D) projects within a team in an industrial or academic laboratory.

**Academic & Research Affiliations**

My primary academic training and research projects were conducted at the University of Angers.

**Key Academic Mentors & Project Supervisors:**

My applied skills were developed under the guidance of several professors who supervised critical projects:

- Image Processing & Computer Vision: Supervised by Pr. [David Rousseau](https://eng-irhs.angers-nantes.hub.inrae.fr/research/imaging-for-horticulture-and-phenotyping/l-equipe/david-rousseau). Guided my work on blood cell image segmentation and object detection using transfer learning, forming my foundation in medical image analysis.

- Generative Models (GANs/VAEs) & NLP Initiation: Supervised by Dr. [Sylvain Lamprier](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=NuGN8SUAAAAJ&hl=en). Oversaw my introductory projects in generative adversarial networks (GANs), variational autoencoders (VAEs), and natural language processing (NLP) for text classification and text generation.

- Reinforcement Learning Initiation: Supervised by Dr. [Sylvain Lamprier](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=NuGN8SUAAAAJ&hl=en). Directed my initial exploration and implementation of Deep Q-Networks (DQN) and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) algorithms.

Master's Thesis (M2): was focusing on the mathematical foundations and applications of diffusion models.

**Academic Projects**

My work is characterized by a dual competency: research on AI models and practical engineering to design functional solutions.

- **Deep Generative Models (Diffusion):** In-depth study and implementation of Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPMs). Exploration of their application to MNIST, linking theoretical research to a (small) concrete use case.

- **Computer Vision for Health:** Application of deep learning models for semantic and instance segmentation of blood cell images, and object detection using transfer learning.

- **Full-Stack Data Applications:** Conception and deployment of a complete web application (Django, SQL) for the visualization and exploration of complex datasets (Enron corpus), demonstrating end-to-end project ownership.

- **Theoretical & Applied ML:** Research on Approximate Bayesian Computation with Random Forests (ABC-RF) and development of predictive models on genomic data.

**Research Interests**

My interests lie at the intersection of algorithmic innovation and applied domains:

- Deep Learning for Medical Image and Signal Processing.
- Generative AI (Diffusion Models, GANs) and their applications in science.

Driven by the pursuit of meaningful impact, I aim to contribute to projects where algorithmic innovation serves a clear societal or industrial purpose. **I am actively seeking a role in industrial R&D, an academic research lab, or a PhD to further pursue these interests.**

This site showcases my projects, from theoretical exploration to implementation. Feel free to explore my work and get in touch to discuss potential collaborations.
